## 001 — UpNFT classification

### Initial interpretation
UpNFT looked like it might be a cohort-2 final-project repository.

### Why
It is a public repository linked from an official UPSide technical article written by a cohort-2 participant.

### Evidence found
The official article identifies UpNFT as an academy practice/assignment protocol used for an audit contest exercise.

### Revised interpretation
`UpNFT` is an **educational assignment artifact**, not one of the four cohort-2 final projects.

### What I learned
A public repo associated with a cohort should not automatically be mapped to the final-project corpus. Artifact type must be verified separately.
